Every single day

There's a rhythm to it.

Children settle when they know what comes next. The order of our day stays the same. Only what we put inside it changes.

  • 1 Morning Circle
  • 2 Breakfast
  • 3 Creative Corner
  • 4 Outdoor Explorers
  • 5 ABC & Number Adventure
  • 6 Music & Movement
  • 7 Lunch
  • 8 Story Time
  • 9 Quiet / Nap Time
  • 10 Evening Snacks & Indoor Play

And some days have their own flavor.

Not on a timetable, and not every week. They turn up when they turn up, which is half the fun of them.

  • Finger painting
  • A book about colors

  • Taste a new fruit or vegetable

  • Where food comes from
  • Practicing table manners
  • Talking about healthy foods

Wonder Wednesday

  • Nature walk
  • Gardening and watering plants
  • Leaf and flower crafts

  • Outdoor science

Creative Kids

  • Puppet show

Fun Friday

  • Dance party

  • Pajama day
