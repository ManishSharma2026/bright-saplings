Allergies are handled seriously here.

If your child has one, this is the part of the page that matters. Tell us at the tour and we will walk you through exactly what we do.

  • Written down before day one. Every allergy, intolerance and dietary restriction is recorded on enrollment and posted in the kitchen where it is cooked, not filed away in a folder.
  • Separated while it's cooked. Separate boards, utensils and prep space for an allergy meal, and it is plated first, before the allergen is anywhere near the counter.
  • Labeled at the table. An allergy plate is named and goes to one child. Nothing is shared off another child's plate, and that rule is enforced by an adult, not by a three-year-old's memory.
  • Nothing new without a parent's say-so. If we want to introduce a food, we ask you first. No surprises, no "we tried it and it was fine".
  • Birthday and treat drop-offs are allowed. You are welcome to bring something in for your child's birthday. Please check with us beforehand so we can work around every allergy in the room, and so nobody has to sit the party out.

Please tell us everything, even the mild ones. A stomach ache we could have avoided still ruins a child's afternoon, and knowing early is always easier than finding out late.
